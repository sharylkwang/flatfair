Common questions
Is it really free?

Yes. Listing, browsing, offers and viewings cost $0. Sellers pay only if they choose a package{% if startingPackage %} (from ${{ startingPackage.price|intcomma }} + GST){% endif %} or an extra service{% if startingAddon %} (from ${{ startingAddon.price|intcomma }} + GST){% endif %}.

Do I still need HDB?

Yes. Register your Intent to Sell on the HDB Flat Portal before you list, at least 7 days before you give an Option to Purchase. FlatFair helps you find the buyer, and you complete the sale through HDB's form and portal.

What does Fair price mean?

The asking price is close to what similar flats nearby sold for, based on HDB resale records. Nobody can pay for this tag.

What does Sponsored mean?

The seller paid to show the flat nearer the top. It says nothing about whether the price is fair.
