In short

  • Listing your flat is free. You pay only for optional packages and services.
  • FlatFair is not a property agency and is not part of HDB. Buyers and sellers complete the sale with HDB themselves.
  • Our price estimates and photo checks are guides, not official valuations.
  • We collect only what we need to run the service, and we never see or store your card details.

By creating an account or using FlatFair, you agree to these terms.

1 About FlatFair

  1. FlatFair is an online marketplace where owners of HDB flats list them for resale and buyers deal with them directly.
  2. FlatFair is a student project built for the IS2108 module at the National University of Singapore. It is not a licensed estate agency, does not act for either side of a sale, and is not connected with the Housing & Development Board (HDB).

2 Your account

  1. You can sign up with your email address or with Google. We confirm your email with a one-time code before your account is used.
  2. Keep your password, recovery codes and authenticator app to yourself. You are responsible for activity on your account.
  3. One account can both buy and sell. Use the Buying and Selling switch to change between them.
  4. We may suspend an account that breaks these terms. We will tell you why.
  5. You can ask to delete your account under Account settings. Our team reviews each request, and your account stays active until then.

3 Listing a flat

  1. List only a flat you own or co-own and are eligible to sell under HDB rules.
  2. Use real, current photos of the flat and accurate details. Photos made or altered with AI are not allowed.
  3. You can have one flat for sale at a time. A paused flat still counts as your flat for sale.
  4. Our team may check a listing before it goes live. We may ask for documents, remove a photo or turn down a listing, and we will always give a reason.
  5. You can change, pause or delete your listing at any time. Deleting it closes open offers and viewings, and we tell the buyers involved.

4 Offers, viewings and the sale

  1. Offers, counter-offers and accepted prices on FlatFair are not legally binding. A sale is agreed only through HDB’s resale process, starting with the Option to Purchase.
  2. Please keep the viewings you book. If you cancel three confirmed viewings within 30 days, booking new viewings is paused for a while.
  3. The deal tracker follows HDB’s resale steps to help you keep on time. HDB’s own instructions and deadlines always apply.

5 Price estimates and automated checks

  1. Our price check, fair-price tags, offer advice and price suggestions come from a model trained on HDB resale prices published on data.gov.sg. They are estimates, not valuations. For loans and grants, HDB’s valuation applies.
  2. Photos are checked automatically. The check may move a photo to the room it shows, ask you to replace a photo, or send it to our team.
  3. Photo search and Shop the look show similar rooms and items for ideas. Items in photos are not part of a sale unless the seller says so.

6 Fees, payments and refunds

  1. Listing a flat is free. Prices for packages and services are shown before GST, and 9% GST is added at checkout.
  2. Packages start as soon as you pay, so they cannot be refunded.
  3. A service can be refunded until our team starts work on it, while its order still says Paid. If you buy a package and a service together, they become two orders, so the service can still be refunded.
  4. To ask for a refund, press Request a refund on the order’s receipt, or message Support. Refunds go back to the card you paid with.
  5. Payments are handled by Stripe. Your card details go straight to Stripe and never reach us.

7 Privacy

What we collect

  • Your name and email address, and your password stored in a scrambled form we cannot read.
  • Your sign-in security settings, such as whether an authenticator app is on.
  • Your listings and photos, offers, messages, viewings, saved flats and saved searches.
  • Your orders and payment records. We never store card numbers.
  • Support messages, and basic technical data such as your IP address for security checks.

How we use it

To run your account, let buyers and sellers deal with each other, check listings and photos, process payments, send the emails you need, and keep FlatFair secure. We do not sell your personal data.

Who can see it

  • Buyers see your flat, never your email, until you accept their offer. Then you both see each other’s name and email.
  • Our team sees what it needs to check listings, handle orders and help you.
  • Services we rely on receive only what they need: Stripe for payments, Google if you sign in with Google, Cloudflare for security checks, and our email provider to send you emails.

Your choices

You can update your name, email and password in Account settings at any time. To get a copy of your data, correct it, or delete your account, message Support. We handle personal data in line with Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Act.

8 Acceptable use

  1. Do not post false or misleading listings, prices or photos.
  2. Be respectful in messages, reviews and the community. Do not share other people’s personal details.
  3. Do not try to get around our checks, payments or security, or use FlatFair to send spam.
  4. We may hide posts or reviews, remove listings or suspend accounts that break these rules.

9 Our responsibility

  1. We work to keep FlatFair accurate and available, but we cannot promise that a flat will sell, at what price, or that the site will always be available.
  2. FlatFair is not a party to any sale. As far as the law allows, we are not responsible for losses arising from dealings between buyers and sellers.

10 Changes and contact

  1. We may update these terms. The date at the top shows when they last changed, and we will tell you about important changes.
  2. Questions about these terms or your data? Message us on the Support page.
