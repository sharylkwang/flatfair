Common questions
Do I need a package?
No. Free listings get offers, viewings and checks too.
Is listing free?
Yes, always. If your price or photos look unusual, our team checks the listing before buyers see it.
Who pays?
Only sellers who pick a package or an extra service. Buyers never pay FlatFair, and HDB’s own fees are separate.
What does Sponsored mean?
The seller paid to show the flat nearer the top. It says nothing about whether the price is fair.
Can I upgrade later?
Yes. Go to My listings and press Upgrade on your flat. The new package starts straight away, and we don’t refund the old one.
Is payment real?
No. This is a demo, so no money is taken.
